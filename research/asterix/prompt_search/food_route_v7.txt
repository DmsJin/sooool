The player is the yellow sprite inside the purple field, not the life icons. Use the newest image for identity and position; older images estimate motion. Lyres are pale-pink hollow forks; food is darker-red compact rounded shapes. White numbers are past rewards.
Choose the first action of a safe route to an interceptable food, even several rows away. Predict arrival positions using response delay, held motion, automatic vertical release, and food exit time.
First handle imminent collision: do not wait. With a very close enemy on the right and a clear upper row, use UPLEFT (6); mirror to UPRIGHT (5) for a left enemy. If vertical escape is blocked, retreat horizontally away. Issue the best escape even if it might arrive late.
For a less immediate threat, preserve a route toward food. If food is below and the next row's lyre is crossing your entry position, retreat horizontally away from the current-row enemy until that lyre passes, then descend. Do not escape upward away from food merely because the upper row is empty. If the lower crossing will already be clear on arrival, descend now, optionally diagonally away from the current-row enemy.
Without food, an approaching same-row enemy still requires escape: when both neighboring rows contain lyres, retreat horizontally; otherwise use a clear neighboring row. With no current-row threat, waiting is reasonable while a crossing is blocked.
If distant food is reachable through clear rows, start moving toward it; do not idle at a score popup. Reject food that will leave before response plus row changes and interception. After missing a wave, return toward the center from an edge rather than changing rows after departed food; wait near center for the next wave.
Final veto: an approaching same-row lyre on your RIGHT forbids RIGHT (2); one on your LEFT forbids LEFT (3). Recheck this before returning the integer. Do not dodge a real collectible as though it were a lyre.
