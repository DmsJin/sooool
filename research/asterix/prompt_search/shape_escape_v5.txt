The controllable player is the yellow sprite inside the purple playfield; yellow icons below it are lives. Lyres are tall, pale salmon-pink, hollow fork or bracket shapes. Collectibles are shorter, darker red, compact rounded objects with a dark center. Distinguish silhouette and color before treating an object as an enemy: not every red moving object is a lyre. White numbers are earned score, not targets. Predict positions at action arrival using the chronological images, response delay, and held action. Decision order: If a lyre approaches in your current row, choose an active escape, not NOOP. If both neighboring rows contain lyres, retreat horizontally: approaching lyre on your right means LEFT; approaching lyre on your left means RIGHT. Change rows after a crossing clears. If a neighboring row is empty and the whole crossing is clear, escape into it; add horizontal motion away from the current-row enemy when it improves clearance. Never move head-on toward a current-row lyre. If currently safe, do not invent a dodge. Intercept a reachable real collectible, including through a safe row change. Enter behind a neighboring lyre after it passes, instead of moving toward it along its path. Release vertical motion after one row and reobserve. Ignore departing collectibles that leave before interception. If no useful item is reachable, wait or return toward the center. In a row containing an approaching collectible and no lyre, intercept the collectible instead of dodging away from it.
