# Fixed mechanics and observed turn reasoning experiment

Two additive candidates retain v9's audit-only forecast fields and the original
five-field GPT action plan. One supplies verified fixed shot and collision rules;
the second additionally asks GPT to distinguish steady drift, deceleration and
observed reversal. No current state, sampled future, boss motion formula, private
fixture identity, reference action or automatic policy is added.

This addresses distinct private diagnostics: a sufficient intercept forecast can
still be followed by insufficient focused movement; an internally consistent
movement can reach an incorrectly extrapolated turning point. These are observed
output errors, not claims about the model's hidden reasoning.

The immutable engine has straight, non-homing shots, an approximately 28.8-pixel
useful firing half-band and small center-based collision bounds in the existing
640x480 screenshots. These tolerances do not guarantee future contact or safety.
GPT still estimates all motion and chooses route, focus, duration and Bomb.

Frozen protocol and tested sources: task artifact kaguya-optimization-v10.
Planned screen: original 26 fixtures, four candidates, two repeats (208 turns),
low/aggressive subscription, 12 workers, shuffle seed1019, no paid API calls.

retain seed719. This reproducibility change preceded all v10 model calls. Wait for v9's existing
confirmation and four-episode batch to terminate before starting this screen.
The old aggregate gate is unchanged. Additionally, inspect positive ordinary

## Completed development screen

All 208 subscription answers were valid; they produced 352 correlated original-
engine counterfactual rollouts. Four sampled clone endpoints matched the original
browser playback. No new candidate passed the unchanged gate. Among delayed
conditions without pre-action loss, baseline v2 had 2 hits, 26 Bomb uses and mean
ordinary-shot damage 51.73; mechanics v10 had 4 / 18 / 56.06 and turn v10 had
11 / 16 / 49.90. Turn v10 also had 6 instantaneous diagnostic hits versus 3 for
baseline. E02 remained at zero ordinary damage for every arm; turn v10 reached
only 2.5 mean damage in E04, and 15 in E08 with one hit. Local shot totals remain
subject to Bomb substitution and genuine early-win truncation. This is tuned
development data, not realtime or held-out evidence; baseline v2 remains default.

The optional low/medium subscription effort control is preparation only; medium
has not been tested in this screen. Experiment labels
`tracking_dodge_safe_intercept_medium_v11` and
`tracking_dodge_mechanics_medium_v11` reuse the exact source strategy strings,
task prompts, cohort wording and output schemas. Their names do not select
effort: an explicit transport override must do that. The new CLI can randomize
these labels in one task queue with `--effort-by-candidate` while preserving the
same screenshots and prompt bytes for each low/medium pair.
