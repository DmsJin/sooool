# Place the falling piece

You know Tetris. Each request shows the game screen (board, HOLD, one NEXT piece, score/lines/level; nothing is
annotated). The piece in play is the falling one, not yet part of the stack; it may already have fallen some rows.
Hollow outlines show where it would land if dropped where it is. Columns are numbered 0-9 from the left.

Reply with exactly one JSON object for the piece in play:

    {"rot": 1, "col": 3, "reason": "Column 4 is one row lower than column 3, so the vertical S sits flush there with no gap."}

- `rot`: 0-3, orientation from the shape table below (rot0 is how the piece spawns; each +1 is a 90 degree clockwise turn).
- `col`: the column number of the LEFTMOST column the piece occupies after rotating. It is dropped straight down there.
- Holes are the main thing to avoid: an empty cell under a filled one stays unusable until every row above it is cleared.
  Before answering, compare several columns and rotations. Prefer a spot where every column under the piece rests
  directly on the stack (no empty cell left underneath), even if it is one or two rows higher.
- Trade-off: clearing two or more rows at once is worth one hole, especially a hole covered by only one row that
  the clears will soon expose. Do not keep a deep well open for a future I piece when the stack next to it is already
  high; fill it with the piece that clears the most rows now.
Gap-free fits. Column height = number of filled rows from the floor up to the top filled cell of that column
(0 if empty). A piece sits with no gap only when the columns it covers have exactly these heights, where h is any number:
  I rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h, h) left to right
  I rot1: needs column heights (h) left to right
  J rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  J rot1: needs column heights (h, h+2) left to right
  J rot2: needs column heights (h+1, h+1, h) left to right
  J rot3: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  L rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  L rot1: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  L rot2: needs column heights (h, h+1, h+1) left to right
  L rot3: needs column heights (h+2, h) left to right
  O rot0: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  S rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h+1) left to right
  S rot1: needs column heights (h+1, h) left to right
  T rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  T rot1: needs column heights (h, h+1) left to right
  T rot2: needs column heights (h+1, h, h+1) left to right
  T rot3: needs column heights (h+1, h) left to right
  Z rot0: needs column heights (h+1, h, h) left to right
  Z rot1: needs column heights (h, h+1) left to right
