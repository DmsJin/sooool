# Task: play single-player Tetris from screenshots

You play Tetris by looking at screenshots and choosing **where the current piece goes**. You cannot press keys; a fixed
program carries out your placements (rotate, slide, hard drop). Goal: **clear as many lines as possible
without topping out.** Keep the stack low and flat, avoid holes, keep the right-hand columns usable for I pieces.

## The screenshot
- The board is 10 columns wide and 20 rows tall. Columns are numbered 0-9 from left to right (the numbers are not drawn).
- The dark-red strip at the top (above the thin red line) is the spawn area; a new piece appears there and falls.
  **The piece in play is the one not yet part of the stack**; in the newest image it may already have fallen some
  rows. Hollow outlines on the stack show where it would land if dropped straight down where it is.
- Right side: HOLD (not used, ignore), NEXT = the one piece that comes after the current one, then SCORE/LINES/LEVEL.
- Colors: I cyan, J blue, L orange, O yellow, S green, T purple, Z red.


## What to send
Place ONLY the piece currently in play (the falling one, not yet part of the stack). Reply with exactly one JSON object:

    {"piece": "S", "rot": 1, "col": 3, "reason": "Column 4 is one row lower than column 3, so the vertical S sits flush there with no gap."}

- `piece`: the letter of the current piece.
- `rot`: 0-3, orientation from the table below (rot0 is how the piece spawns; each +1 is a 90 degree clockwise turn).
- `col`: the column number of the LEFTMOST column the piece occupies after rotating.
- The piece is dropped straight down in that column span.
- Holes are the main thing to avoid. A hole is an empty cell with a filled cell above it; it cannot be filled later.
  Before answering, compare several columns and rotations. If ANY spot lets every column under the piece rest
  directly on the stack (no empty cell left underneath), choose such a spot, even if it is one or two rows higher.
  Accept a gap only when no gap-free spot exists anywhere.
Gap-free fits. Column height = number of filled rows from the floor up to the top filled cell of that column
(0 if empty). A piece sits with no gap only when the columns it covers have exactly these heights, where h is any number:
  I rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h, h) left to right
  I rot1: needs column heights (h) left to right
  J rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  J rot1: needs column heights (h, h+2) left to right
  J rot2: needs column heights (h+1, h+1, h) left to right
  J rot3: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  L rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  L rot1: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  L rot2: needs column heights (h, h+1, h+1) left to right
  L rot3: needs column heights (h+2, h) left to right
  O rot0: needs column heights (h, h) left to right
  S rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h+1) left to right
  S rot1: needs column heights (h+1, h) left to right
  T rot0: needs column heights (h, h, h) left to right
  T rot1: needs column heights (h, h+1) left to right
  T rot2: needs column heights (h+1, h, h+1) left to right
  T rot3: needs column heights (h+1, h) left to right
  Z rot0: needs column heights (h+1, h, h) left to right
  Z rot1: needs column heights (h, h+1) left to right
