FIXTURE PLACEMENT PROTOCOL (every step is your own literal command; nothing is automatic):
1. Measure the grasp. As soon as vacuum_ok is true, use the wrist image: find the held board's near edge (the edge closest to the image top-centre region). Its midpoint offset from the centred-board reference and its slope give the board-centre offset from the TCP in tool axes and the board yaw relative to the tool. Keep both in memory (mm, rad).
2. Aim the BOARD, not the TCP. TCP target XY = fixture centre [0,-0.08] minus the grasp offset rotated into world axes; tool yaw target = current tool yaw minus the board's world yaw, so the board ends at yaw 0.
   Orientation matters: yaw 0 means the board's silver USB connector points to world +x (image right in the top view, toward the magazines). An edge slope only gives yaw modulo pi; use the USB connector to decide between theta and theta+pi. A board seated at yaw pi fits the pocket but is NOT present.
3. Hover. Carry at z >= 0.90, then descend to TCP z = 0.8010 (board about 10 mm above the seat). End that chunk with a zero-motion hold of at least 10 commands so the arm settles. Keep vacuum on.
4. Check and correct. In the next observation compare the held board's near edge with the two white locator pins of the fixture pocket in the wrist image (also the top image). Correct residual XY/yaw with steps of at most 0.002 m / 0.02 rad, then hold at least 10 commands.
5. Seat. Descend at most 0.003 m per command to TCP z = 0.7912 (board centre 0.7908 is seated), hold 10 commands, then send vacuum=false in its own zero-motion command, hold 6 more commands, then raise to z >= 0.85.
6. Verify. fixture_board_present must be true before you close the fixture. If it is false, locate the board, regrasp at its centre and repeat steps 1-5. If the board already lies between the locator pins but is not present, it is probably reversed: lift to z >= 0.85, rotate the tool by pi (16 commands of 0.196 rad), then reseat.
Never release while moving. Never descend and release within the same segment.
