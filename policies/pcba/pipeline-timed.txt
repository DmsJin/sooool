TIMED INTERCEPTION (replaces cycle steps A and B; target about 20 s per board).
start_at_s: you may set start_at_s (simulation seconds, the same clock as capture_time_s). The robot holds still until then and command k of your chunk runs at start_at_s + 0.05*k. A reply that arrives after its start_at_s is discarded, so choose start_at_s >= capture_time_s + reply_latency_s.p90_s + 0.4 (use 5.0 s if no statistics yet), and not earlier than the committed chunk's end. Otherwise set start_at_s null.
Predict board motion from the capture time (all public): the belt moves every board at exactly 0.055 m/s along the loop, counter-clockwise from above, and keeps each board's lateral offset. Right straight (x near -0.36, -0.18 < y < 0.18): x stays constant, y(T) = y_capture + 0.055 * (T - capture_time_s). Lower curve (y < -0.18): the board turns counter-clockwise about [-0.54, -0.18] at 0.3056 rad/s at its own radius, then enters the right straight at y = -0.18.
Orientation first: in the top image find the target's silver USB connector (one end of the board). The board's yaw is the world angle of the direction from its centre towards that end (yaw 0 = USB towards +x). Boards keep their yaw along the right straight.
Intercept: pick the board whose centre you can meet soonest. Choose a meeting time T and point P = predicted board centre at T (on the right straight, y between -0.12 and +0.12). In ONE chunk with start_at_s set: move at z >= 0.83 to above P while turning the tool to the target's yaw (so the caught board sits square to the tool), descend to z = 0.8040 with vacuum=true so the cup is still at P by T - 0.4 s, hold still until T + 0.6 s (the board's centre passes under the cup at T; the hold covers timing error), then lift to z >= 0.85, carry to the fixture while turning the tool back to yaw 0 (the board then has its USB towards +x), descend to hover z = 0.8010 and hold 8. Do not wait for a reply between catching and carrying. Never rotate a held board by pi; only correct the small residual skew you measure at the hover.
At the hover reply: if vacuum_ok is false, the catch failed: raise and plan the next timed interception. If true, measure the grasp in the wrist image and continue with cycle step C.
While a sorting chunk (cycle step D) is committed, plan the next timed interception from the expected end pose, so the robot never idles at the belt.
Sort a board only after its own published result (result board_id equals the board you hold); never guess a result.
