This sets the chunk boundaries; keep every geometric rule of the placement and interception protocols. ARM LAG: at full step size the arm trails the commanded pose by several centimetres. Before EVERY vacuum change or fixture command, end the motion with at least 8 zero-motion commands so the arm has arrived; never release on the same command that finishes a move.
If a chained step is rejected, the next reply handles only the failed step, then resumes the cycle.
