THROUGHPUT CYCLE, TWO REPLIES PER BOARD (goal: all five boards tested and correctly sorted as early as possible; every reply costs about 4 s, so chain every step whose outcome you already know). This sets the chunk boundaries; keep every geometric rule of the placement and interception protocols, except that the hover check is folded into chunk B. The executor stops a chunk at the first rejected command, so a chained step whose condition is not met aborts safely and you see the receipt next time.
A. Ambush as in the interception protocol. Hold 80-120 commands per chunk. Prefer the board that will reach your ambush point soonest. On a miss, in the same chunk raise to z >= 0.83 and move straight to the next ambush pose.
B. Reply that first sees vacuum_ok: measure the grasp offset and relative yaw precisely in the wrist image (the board's near edge, 0.345 mm/px), compute the TCP target that puts the BOARD centre on the fixture centre at yaw 0 (USB to +x), then in ONE chunk: lift to z >= 0.85, carry and rotate, descend to z = 0.8010, hold 8, descend to z = 0.7912 (<= 0.003 m per command), hold 8, vacuum=false alone, hold 4, raise 0.02 m, hold 2, fixture close, hold 8, fixture start_test. Use start_guard vacuum_ok=true. If close is rejected (board not seated), the next reply regrasps and reseats.
Never send a chunk that only waits for a test result. If your observation shows a test started but no new result yet, chain the result-independent part instead: (after the committed chunk) hold 30 so the test finishes, fixture open, hold 8, descend 0.02 m, hold 2, vacuum=true, hold 6, raise to z >= 0.85 and move to the output staging point x = 0.43, y = 0.05, z = 0.90, hold 8. The next reply then only chooses and fills the output.
C. Reply that sees the published result: in ONE chunk fixture open, hold 8, descend 0.02 m, hold 2, vacuum=true, hold 6, raise to z >= 0.85, carry to the matching empty output, insert, release, withdraw, then move to the next ambush pose at z >= 0.83. Keep the tool yaw unchanged from the regrasp until release (the board is then at yaw 0). PASS/FAIL magazines: approach at y = 0.045 at slot z + 0.006, push until the BOARD centre is at y = 0.210 (TCP y = 0.210 minus the grasp offset along world y) with steps of at most 0.01 m for the last 0.06 m, hold 10, release, hold 4, withdraw along -y to y = 0.045 at the same height, then rise. ERROR tray: BOARD centre on the tray centre, descend to z = 0.769, hold 10, release. Use start_guard tester_state=complete and the published result_test_id.
ARM LAG: at full step size the arm trails the commanded pose by several centimetres. Before EVERY vacuum change or fixture command, end the motion with at least 8 zero-motion commands; never release on the same command that finishes a move.
If a chained step is rejected, the next reply handles only the failed step, then resumes the cycle.
